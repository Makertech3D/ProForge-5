## Getting Started

Before installing any community mod, please make sure you have:

- Backed up your current printer configuration and settings
- Verified your firmware version is compatible with the mod
- Reviewed the mod's requirements and difficulty rating

> ⚠️ **Note:** Community mods are not officially supported by ProForge. Install at your own risk and always follow the safety guidelines below.

## Mod Categories

### 🖨️ Print Quality
Mods focused on improving layer adhesion, surface finish, accuracy, and overall output quality.

### ⚙️ Mechanical Upgrades
Hardware modifications including motion system improvements, frame bracing, vibration dampening, and component replacements.

### 🌡️ Thermal & Cooling
Hotend upgrades, part cooling enhancements, enclosure mods, and heated chamber improvements.

### 📦 Extruder & Feeder
Direct drive conversions, dual extrusion setups, filament sensor mounts, and runout detection mods.

### 🔌 Electronics & Wiring
Board replacements, cable management solutions, lighting, and power supply modifications.

### 💻 Software & Firmware
Custom firmware builds, slicer profiles, macros, and configuration files.

### 🗂️ Organization & Mounts
Tool holders, spool mounts, filament guides, camera mounts, and other utility accessories.

### 🎨 Cosmetic
Aesthetic mods, custom panels, logos, and visual upgrades.

## How to Submit a Mod

1. **Fork** this repository
2. Create a new folder under the relevant category using the naming convention:
   ```
   /community-mods/<category>/<your-mod-name>/
   ```
3. Include all required files (see [Mod Template](#mod-template) below)
4. Open a **Pull Request** with a clear title and description
5. Respond to any review feedback from maintainers

> Please ensure your mod has been personally tested on a ProForge 5 before submitting.

---

## Mod Template

Each mod submission **must** include the following structure:

```
your-mod-name/
├── README.md          # Description, instructions, photos
├── files/             # STL, CAD, config files, etc.
│   └── ...
├── images/            # Photos or renders of the mod
│   └── ...
└── CHANGELOG.md       # Version history (optional but encouraged)
```

### Required in your mod's `README.md`:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Author** | Your GitHub username |
| **Version** | e.g. `v1.0.0` |
| **Firmware Compatibility** | Tested firmware version(s) |
| **Difficulty** | Easy / Medium / Hard |
| **Print Time** | Estimated print time (if applicable) |
| **Materials Needed** | Hardware, filament type, tools |
| **Description** | What the mod does and why it's useful |
| **Installation Instructions** | Step-by-step guide |
| **Known Issues** | Any caveats or limitations |

## Safety & Disclaimer

- Always **power off** your printer before making hardware modifications
- **Community mods are not endorsed or tested by ProForge** — use them at your own risk
- Electrical modifications may **void your warranty** — proceed with caution
- Never leave a modified printer unattended during its first test print
- If a mod requires disabling safety features, consider the risks carefully

The maintainers of this repository are **not responsible** for any damage to your printer, property, or injury resulting from the use of community mods.

## Contributing Guidelines

To keep this repository clean and useful for everyone:

- ✅ Test your mod before submitting
- ✅ Use clear, descriptive names for files and folders
- ✅ Write instructions a beginner can follow
- ✅ Include photos or renders where possible
- ✅ Keep files reasonably sized (use releases for large assets)
- ❌ Do not submit mods that are untested or incomplete
- ❌ Do not plagiarise others' work without credit
- ❌ Do not include copyrighted material without permission
